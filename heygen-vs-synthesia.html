HeyGen vs Synthesia in 2026: Which AI Video Generator Should You Use?

Updated September 2026 · 10 min read

Quick answer: Synthesia is now the better value for most individuals and small businesses after its early-2026 price cuts and the addition of AI Playground (built-in Veo 3.1 and Sora 2 access for B-roll). HeyGen still wins on avatar realism and speed if budget is less of a concern, but its move to a fully credit-based model in May 2026 makes real costs harder to predict.

Both platforms generate realistic talking-head avatar videos from a script, without cameras or actors, and both have changed significantly in 2026. The comparisons still circulating from last year are largely out of date.

What Changed in 2026

  • HeyGen retired its unlimited plans on May 15, 2026, moving entirely to credit-based pricing. Every feature now consumes credits, including standard videos that used to be unlimited, which means the advertised monthly price no longer reflects what heavy users actually pay.
  • Synthesia cut prices in early 2026: its Starter plan dropped from $19/month to $14/month billed annually, and Creator dropped from $89/month to $59/month.
  • Synthesia added AI Playground, giving direct in-editor access to Google's Veo 3.1 and OpenAI's Sora 2 for generating B-roll footage, available even on the free plan.

Who Should Pick HeyGen

Pick HeyGen if avatar realism and speed of custom avatar creation matter more than predictable monthly cost. It remains the stronger choice for creators who want the most human-looking output and don't mind budgeting for credit top-ups.

FAQ

Is HeyGen or Synthesia cheaper?
On paper, both start around $29/month, but Synthesia's 2026 price cuts and clearer video-minute caps make its real cost more predictable. HeyGen's credit system means the effective price for heavy users is often higher than the sticker price suggests.

Which has better avatars?
HeyGen is generally rated higher for realism, particularly with its Avatar IV and Instant Avatar features.

Can I use either for free?
Yes, both offer a free plan with no credit card required, though limits are tight enough to only really work as a test drive.
