Best Free AI Girlfriend Apps (No Credit Card Needed)

Most "free" AI girlfriend apps aren't really free — they're a demo with a message counter. Here's which ones give you an actual usable free tier, and which ones are just a trial with extra steps.

Almost every AI girlfriend app advertises a free tier. Almost none of them tell you upfront how limited it actually is. Some cap you at 10-20 messages before the paywall hits — barely enough to say hello. Others give you a genuinely usable daily allowance you can return to without paying a cent.

We looked past the marketing pages and checked what each free tier actually includes: how many messages you get, whether images and voice are locked, and how quickly you hit a wall. No credit card should be required for any of these — if a site asks for payment details just to "unlock" a free trial, that's a red flag, not a free tier.

Nomi AI — best free tier for memory-focused chat

Nomi's free plan gives you one companion and a limited number of daily messages — no voice calls or image generation on the free tier, and the cap is tight enough that it works better as a taste-test than daily use. Where Nomi stands out is what happens after you commit: its memory system is consistently rated among the strongest in the category, so if the free tier convinces you the conversation quality is there, the jump to paid (roughly $8–17/month depending on billing cycle) buys a genuinely deep, long-term companion rather than a gimmick.

CandyAI's free plan is closer to a trial than an ongoing free product — expect somewhere around 10–20 messages before you're prompted to upgrade, with images watermarked or locked entirely. What "free" actually means across these apps

A pattern shows up once you compare enough of these side by side: the free tier is rarely about the core chat experience — it's almost always about limiting volume (daily messages) or modality (locking voice, images, or video behind payment). Text-only conversation is the most commonly "free" feature; images and voice are where nearly every app starts charging.

If a free tier feels too good to be true — unlimited everything, no catch — check the privacy policy before you get attached to a companion. Free products in this space are sometimes funded by using conversations for AI training or advertising, not just conversion to paid plans.

How we'd choose

If you want to actually evaluate memory and conversation depth before paying, Nomi's free tier — despite the low cap — gives you a real sample of the thing you're actually paying for. If your priority is seeing what the image generation looks like before committing, CandyAI's trial (even watermarked) shows you the visual quality upfront.
